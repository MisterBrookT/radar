# Historical user story — daily prototype

Superseded by the [topic-adaptive weekly playbook](briefing-playbook.md). Kept as design history, not current requirements.

I have little time and don't want paper details. Each day I watch one short video (~10 min) that tells me what people in generative UI are doing — researchers and builders — and sparks ideas.

## What I get

- One video per day, delivered to WeChat.
- It covers ~5 sources (papers + X posts), **grouped into 2–3 stories**, not listed one by one.
- Shape: today's main pattern → stories (what was built, why it's interesting, what's unproven) → one idea worth carrying forward.
- Source links and their attention signals are attached, so I can dig in if I want.

## Rules

- Topic: generative UI (AI systems that generate or adapt interfaces).
- Selection relies on platform signals (X engagement, alphaXiv popularity), not our own novelty judgment.
- Tone: idea briefing — connections and implications, with reported results kept separate from speculation.
- A quiet day gives a shorter video, not filler.

## Not in scope (v1)

- Other topics, full paper summaries, interactive Q&A.
